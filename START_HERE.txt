NEW ADMIN COMMAND
=================
/zenith detect <category>

The category auto-fills:
ender
ravager
guardian
warden
wither
zenith

Examples:
/zenith detect ender
/zenith detect warden
/zenith detect zenith

WHAT IT CHECKS
==============
- ONLINE player inventories
- ONLINE player Ender Chests
- loaded dropped Boss Blade item entities

It also tells you whether that Boss Blade craft lock is currently:
LOCKED
or
AVAILABLE

IMPORTANT
=========
This is a MANUAL inspection tool, not an automatic reset.

If the command says the blade is not found, that does NOT prove the blade was
destroyed. It may still be in:
- an offline player's inventory
- an offline player's Ender Chest
- a chest or barrel
- a shulker box
- an unloaded chunk

That is why /zenith resetboss <category> remains the final owner/admin decision.
