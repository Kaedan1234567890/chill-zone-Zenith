NEW IN FIX 9
============
1. /zenith give now supports a quantity:
      /zenith give @s ender_essence 32
      /zenith give Kaedan ender_blade 1
      /zenith give @p ravager_horn 16

2. Commands remain administrator/gamemaster-only.

3. All sword names now have custom colours.
4. Every sword has tooltip lore describing its right-click ability and cooldown.
5. Right-click ability PROTOTYPES are now wired in for every sword.
6. Ability use respects branch activation.
7. Boss Blade destruction handling:
   - If a legitimately crafted Boss Blade becomes a dropped ItemEntity and is
     actually destroyed (fire/explosion/etc.), that branch's one-per-world lock
     resets automatically.
   - Putting it in an inventory, Ender Chest, chest, shulker, or logging out does
     NOT trigger the destruction callback and does NOT unlock another craft.
   - Admin /zenith resetboss <branch> remains available as the testing/manual
     recovery command, especially for CREATIVE inventory deletion, which does not
     create a destroyed ItemEntity for the server to observe.
