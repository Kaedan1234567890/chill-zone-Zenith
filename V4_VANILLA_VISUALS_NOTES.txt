ZENITH v4 - VANILLA VISUALS / NO RESOURCE PACK

Goal:
Keep the mod fully server-side and eliminate broken/missing custom textures.

Client visuals:
- Intermediate Zenith swords -> Diamond Sword
- Golden Desire -> Golden Sword
- Boss Blades -> Netherite Sword
- Zenith Blade -> Netherite Sword
- All Zenith crafting tables -> normal Crafting Table

The custom names, name colours, lore, abilities, cooldowns, commands,
recipes, progression, and unique-boss rules remain server-side.

This version DOES NOT force a Polymer resource pack.
That means there should be no giant purple/black missing-model blocks from
the Zenith custom assets.

The different swords are still identified by their custom names and coloured
text. Boss Blades keep their star names, and the Zenith keeps its triple-star
name.

This is intentionally the reliable vanilla-visual fallback.
