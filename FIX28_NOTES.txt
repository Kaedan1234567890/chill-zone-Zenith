CHILL ZONE ZENITH - FIX 28
VANILLA-SCALE SWORD TEXTURE TEST BUILD

Built directly on Fix 27.

TEXTURE CHANGE
==============
All 18 Zenith sword textures were replaced with the corrected
Vanilla-Scale V2 sprites.

Scale target:
- Standard Minecraft 16x16 item texture canvas.
- Regular Zenith swords use approximately 15 pixels across their
  longest visible dimension.
- Boss Blades and Zenith can use approximately the full 16-pixel
  dimension.
- Nearest-neighbour pixel scaling only.
- No smoothing or anti-aliasing.

The goal of this build is to test the IN-GAME apparent size against
normal Minecraft swords before doing the final artistic redesign.

Everything from Fix 27 is preserved:
- ★ Boss Blade naming
- ★★★ Zenith naming
- bold branch-coloured custom names
- descriptions and cooldown text
- reconnect cooldown protection
- /zenith test <player>, /zenith untest <player>
- /zenith test all, /zenith untest all
- abilities, sounds, drops, locks, activation, detect/reset commands

IMPORTANT
=========
This is a visual-scale test build. The custom silhouettes/details are
still the current art and are intended to be redesigned after the scale
is confirmed in-game.
