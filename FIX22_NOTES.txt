CHILL ZONE ZENITH - FIX 22

Fixes Minecraft Java 26.2 compile errors in ZenithMobDrops.java.

Problem:
Minecraft 26.2 in this mapping/API does not expose mob constants such as
EntityType.ENDERMAN, EntityType.SHULKER, EntityType.RAVAGER, etc.

Fix:
The death listener now reads the killed entity's registered Minecraft ID
(enderman, shulker, ravager, etc.) from BuiltInRegistries.ENTITY_TYPE and
matches that ID. Drop rates and branch activation gating are unchanged.

Everything from Fix 21 / Fix 20 remains included.
