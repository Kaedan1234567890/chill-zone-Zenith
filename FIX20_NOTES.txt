CHILL ZONE ZENITH - FIX 20 ABILITY REWORK

Built on Fix 19, so the custom mob-drop listener remains included.

COOLDOWNS
- Mini/intermediate swords: 20 seconds
- Boss Blades: 40 seconds
- Zenith Blade: 5 minutes (300 seconds)

TEST COMMANDS (gamemaster/admin only)
- /zenith test   -> ignores Zenith sword cooldowns for the player running it
- /zenith untest -> restores normal cooldown behaviour

REWORK HIGHLIGHTS
- Tooltips now explicitly explain what right-click does and show cooldown.
- Ender Blade: 4-block utility blink.
- Shulker Blade: escape blink backwards + sideways, no attack.
- Ender Dragon Blade: moving = 12-block damaging warp; standing = breath burst + escape.
- Sword of Undying: Absorption VIII for 8 sec.
- Mansion Blade: Speed III + Strength III for 8 sec.
- Ravager Blade: ram + heavy hit + 5 sec Speed III/Strength III.
- Prismarine Blade: Mining Fatigue shot, no direct damage.
- Sponge Blade: wave knockback, no direct damage.
- Elder Tide Blade: nearby targets in water lose air immediately.
- Elder Guardian Blade: strong knockback + Mining Fatigue + Slowness, no direct damage.
- Echo Blade: keeps 18-block reveal.
- Warden's Wrath: weakened sonic damage (~4 hits unarmoured).
- Warden Blade: delayed reveal + sonic beam, tuned toward ~2 hits unarmoured.
- Blade of Fire: fire-only area effect.
- Golden Desire: keeps speed/haste utility.
- Ghost Blade: mini non-terrain-destroying fire shot, 2.5 hearts direct damage + fire.
- Wither Blade: one focused Wither-skull style hit, ~half base health unarmoured.
- Zenith: lethal 15-lane storm remains; Totems are not deliberately bypassed.

SOUNDS
Initial vanilla sound pass is included for several signature abilities (Ender, Shulker,
Dragon, Totem, Illager, Ravager, Guardian, Warden/Wither family). This can be expanded
and tuned after runtime testing.

IMPORTANT
Damage numbers use normal Minecraft damage handling, so armour can reduce normal
ability damage. Zenith remains intentionally extreme/lethal.

The branch-specific recipe-display GUI discussed previously is NOT included in this
file yet; this update focuses on the requested ability/cooldown/lore rework while
preserving Fix 19 mob drops.
