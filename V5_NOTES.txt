ZENITH v5 - DIAMOND/NETHERITE VISUALS + NORMAL SWORD ENCHANTING

Changes from v4:
- Golden Desire now displays as a Diamond Sword, not a Golden Sword.
- No Zenith weapon uses a Gold Sword client appearance.
- Intermediate blades display as Diamond Swords.
- Boss Blades display as Netherite Swords.
- Zenith Blade displays as a Netherite Sword.
- Crafting tables still display as normal vanilla Crafting Tables.

Enchanting:
The Zenith weapons keep normal sword enchanting support. Players can use
the sword enchantments Minecraft normally allows for these weapon items,
including examples such as Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods,
Unbreaking, Mending, Fire Aspect, Knockback, and Looting where normal
Minecraft compatibility rules allow them.

No custom code was added to strip or block enchantments.

Abilities, commands, cooldowns, recipes, progression, custom names/lore,
and server-side-only Polymer representation remain unchanged.
